1. INTRODUCTION
JC Smart Ride is a smart campus carpooling web application proposed for the students
of J.C. Bose University of Science and Technology, YMCA, Faridabad. Its main
purpose is to help students travelling on similar routes and at similar times find suitable
carpooling partners.
The system has two main users: Driver and Passenger. Drivers can add ride details such
as pickup location, destination, date, time, available seats, and cost-sharing information.
Passengers can search for rides based on their source, destination, date, and preferred
time.A Smart Match module analyzes available rides and provides a matching score to
recommend the most suitable ride. The project also includes a KNN-based
recommendation system using Python and Scikit-learn to improve ride matching.
The application will be developed as a responsive website for both mobile phones and
laptops, using React.js for the frontend, Python Flask for the backend, MySQL for the
database, and Python/Scikit-learn for smart recommendations.
The main objective of JC Smart Ride is to reduce travel costs, utilize empty vehicle
seats efficiently, reduce unnecessary vehicle movement, and provide a safe and
organized university-specific carpooling platform..
2. PROBLEM IDENTIFICATION AND MOTIVATION
Students often travel to and from the university through similar routes but may not
know which other students are travelling at the same time. Students with cars may have
empty seats, while students without a vehicle may need an affordable and convenient
ride. Informal methods such as asking friends or sending messages in groups do not
provide a proper search, matching, booking and ride-management system.
The major problems identified for the proposed project are:
• Difficulty in finding a ride that matches the required pickup location, destination
and time.
• Unused seats in private vehicles while travelling to the university or back home.
• Higher individual travel cost when students travel alone.
• Lack of a university-focused platform for organized ride sharing.

• Difficulty comparing multiple available rides on route, time, distance, cost and
seat availability.
• Limited safety and trust when users do not have a proper student verification
mechanism.
• No single place to manage upcoming rides, completed rides, cancelled rides and
ride requests.
These problems motivate the development of JC Smart Ride as a simple but expandable
campus mobility system that connects students with suitable rides while keeping the
process organized and easier to manage.
